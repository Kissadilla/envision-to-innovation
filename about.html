About us

Who we are

Envision to Innovation is a student-led nonprofit working to close the digital divide.

We believe every student deserves access to the tools that shape the future, no matter where they go to school.

We build technology hubs in underserved rural schools, starting in Mexico. Setting up equipment is only the beginning. We also teach the skills to use it and work with the school and its families, so the hub keeps serving students long after we leave.

We're students who saw a gap and decided to do something about it. Our goal is to connect rural classrooms to the opportunities technology opens up, one school at a time.

Working note

Field question: what does a hub need to keep working after we leave?

  1. Infrastructure. Devices and connectivity students can learn with.
  2. Curriculum. AI and digital literacy workshops that turn access into skills.
  3. Community. Local schools and families who carry the work forward.
