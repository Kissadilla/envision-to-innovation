Closing the digital divide

Every student deserves the tools that shape the future, no matter where they go to school.

We are a student-led nonprofit that builds technology hubs in underserved rural schools, and makes sure they last.

What a hub needs to last

Our work stands on three pillars.

  • Infrastructure

    Setting up the devices and connectivity students need to learn with technology.

  • Curriculum

    Running AI and digital literacy workshops that turn access into real skills.

  • Community

    Partnering with local schools and families so the impact keeps growing after we leave.

Why we started

We're students who saw a gap and decided to do something about it.

Our goal is to connect rural classrooms to the opportunities technology opens up, one school at a time.
