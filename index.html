How a hub lasts

Our work stands on three pillars.

Infrastructure

Setting up the devices and connectivity students need to learn with technology.

Curriculum

Running AI and digital literacy workshops that turn access into real skills.

Community

Partnering with local schools and families so the impact keeps growing after we leave.

Who we are

We're students who saw a gap.

Envision to Innovation is a student-led nonprofit working to close the digital divide. We believe every student deserves access to the tools that shape the future, no matter where they go to school.

Our goal is to connect rural classrooms to the opportunities technology opens up, one school at a time.

Working note

Field question

What does a hub need to keep working after we leave?

  1. Infrastructure. Devices and connectivity students can learn with.
  2. Curriculum. AI and digital literacy workshops that turn access into skills.
  3. Community. Local schools and families who carry the work forward.
